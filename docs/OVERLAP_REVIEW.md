# Overlap Review

## 0.1.0-dev.1 scope

The first block supports only **Grapple** and **Trip** through Athletics skill-check criticals.

### Critical Forge Core

Critical Forge Core currently supplies attack, spell-attack, saving-throw, and generic fallback material, but no action-specific Grapple or Trip skill cards. This block therefore occupies the new `skillCheckCriticalSuccess` / `skillCheckCriticalFailure` space directly.

### Goblin Engineering

Goblin Engineering's skill cards are equipment-centered and currently target Crafting, Thievery, and Medicine actions such as Repair, Craft, Pick a Lock, Disable a Device, and medical treatment. The Physical Actions block has no skill/action overlap with those cards.

### Martial Consequences and attack packs

Grapple and Trip are Athletics actions, not Strike criticals. Skillful Consequences does not reuse weapon-fumble themes such as lost grip, firing rhythm, or weapon malfunction.

## Design separation inside the block

The Grapple cards focus on leverage, clinch commitment, follow-up holds, and teamwork around a controlled target.

The Trip cards focus on footing, follow-through, recovery, and ground-position advantage.

The cards deliberately avoid replacing the normal PF2e critical success or critical failure for either action.
