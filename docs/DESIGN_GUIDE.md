# Skillful Consequences Design Guide

## Core rule

Every card begins after the normal PF2e result.

A card may add a follow-up, opening, complication, clue, short modifier, movement opportunity, or narrative development. It should not downgrade a critical success, erase a critical failure, or silently rewrite the action itself.

## Action-specific identity

Cards should answer: **What is interesting about this exact action going exceptionally well or badly?**

A generic `+1 to your next check` should be rare unless the follow-up is tightly tied to the same target, action family, or fictional situation.

## Mechanical weight

For combat skill actions:

- `narrative`: no additional mechanical effect;
- `light`: small one-use modifier or narrow brief advantage/setback;
- `moderate`: brief action-economy, reaction, movement, or defense consequence;
- `strong`: exceptional and uncommon; avoid by default because the PF2e critical result is already significant.

## Multiple cards per action

Frequent actions should generally receive six cards: three critical successes and three critical failures. The six should not merely be stronger and weaker versions of the same modifier.

A useful mix is:

- one follow-up or setup card;
- one positioning/defense/teamwork card;
- one narrative or unusual card.

## Other expansions

Skillful Consequences focuses on the **action and its aftermath**.

- Goblin Engineering focuses on equipment developing problems or strange side effects.
- Martial Consequences and other attack packs focus on weapon attacks and combat criticals.
- The normal PF2e result remains authoritative in all cases.
